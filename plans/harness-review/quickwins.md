For the next live capture, [Step 08](steps/08-stop-on-shadow-result.md) targets the measured 202 s tail after case publication. It requires a new lifecycle test before enabling the earlier stop. Lowering every capture timeout would risk truncating slower scenes.

**Sequence.** Q1 and Q2 first. Q3 and Q6 improve the next pilot even if the renderer stays unchanged. Q4 and Q5 have independent, cheap counterexamples. Defer general CI sharding and broad documentation movement until the paired benchmarks show where the time goes. The precision experiment in [05](steps/05-controlled-replay.md) depends on controlling shader and runtime identity; treating it as a quick fix would spend another replay without establishing causality.
