# R0 disposition and PR sequence

This map proposes what to maintain, consolidate, relocate, archive, and remove at the pinned R0 tree. Approval accepts the sequence and the listed conditions. A missing dependency or failed restore keeps its item in place. Paths outside the reviewed rows keep their current disposition until a PR inventories its exact affected files.

[R0 audit](r0-audit.md) · [R0 measurements](r0-measurements.md)

The audit-local `path-dispositions.csv` expands the named rules and includes the static consumers and references for all 2,388 paths. It contains 902 keep, 25 keep frozen, 91 consolidate, 25 relocate, four retain-finding, three archive candidates, and seven delete candidates. The remaining 1,331 paths are held pending consumer and evidence review. The CSV's SHA-256 is `624da32de51161241ca63e492169a486d1ff8d2922bd7c89ba2052119ed80320`.
