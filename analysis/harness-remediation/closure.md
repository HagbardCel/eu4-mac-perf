## Scientific boundary

The harness-integrity work is complete; the GL-control scientific question is
not, and is not presented as one.

- The historical present-904 GL control remains **unqualified**: 5 different
  pixels at checkpoint 15 and 4,645 at checkpoint 16 against live GL, all four
  channels compared, `metal_blame_allowed=false`.
- The 904 seal does not contain the default drawable's `GL_SAMPLES`,
  `GL_SAMPLE_BUFFERS` or bit depths, nor prior-frame contents. A state that was
  never observed cannot be recovered from that seal. The 904 bytes and
  measurements are preserved unchanged; do not create a new capture carrying
  the 904 seal.
- The authentic compressed-vs-decoded comparison is complete for 904: the
  original block path reproduces the shadow byte-for-byte and is much closer to
  live GL than the decoded fallback (4 vs 858 pixels at checkpoint 15). This
  identifies texture representation as material, not as the cause of the
  remaining 4 pixels.
- A new control experiment, **if authorized**, requires: (1) a newly sealed
  capture that records `GL_SAMPLES`, `GL_SAMPLE_BUFFERS`, depth/stencil/color
  bit depths for the default drawable and the prior-frame contents; (2) a new
  original-API renderer that renders from that capture's own stream, resources
  and checkpoint, with a receipt bound to a registered renderer *before*
  `AUTHORITIES` is populated; (3) exact four-channel equality at the first
  divergent checkpoint before any Metal blame. Only zero differing BGRA bytes
  permit the residual to be considered renderer-side. Never enable Metal blame
  on a self-attested control.
