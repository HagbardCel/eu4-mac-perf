Repair `candidate.c` so that it reproduces the pinned behaviour of
`synth_apply` (Oracle function id `synth:apply`), the function declared in
`synth.h`, as evidenced by the visible fixtures under `fixtures/visible/`.

An Oracle tool server exposes five direct tools:

- `oracle_inspect({"function_id": "synth:apply"})` - static evidence and the
  ABI/state layout.
- `oracle_fixtures({"function_id": "synth:apply"})` - the visible fixtures.
- `oracle_coverage({"function_id": "synth:apply"})` - counts and observable
  domains.
- `oracle_test({"function_id": "synth:apply", "candidate_path": "candidate.c"})`
  - compiles `candidate.c` and runs the visible fixtures; returns a
  `test_run_id`.
- `oracle_diff({"run_id": "<test_run_id>"})` - the first observable divergence,
  and the list of failing fixtures.

`function_id` and `candidate_path` are both required, and `candidate_path` is
relative to your working directory.

Rules:

- Change only `candidate.c`. Do not create, delete or rename any other file.
- Do not shell out, and do not build or run anything yourself; take every
  observation through `oracle_test` and `oracle_diff`.
- Work until `oracle_test` reports that every visible fixture passes, then stop.
