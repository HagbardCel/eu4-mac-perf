# E2 — independent adversarial certification audit

## Deliverable

Adversarial fixtures and an independent audit of E1's implementation and results.
E2 may design the tests as soon as the E result and admission contracts are frozen;
the final acceptance audit waits for E1 to be implemented.

E2 must not implement its own certifier, rewrite E1, or infer correctness from E1's
reported status. It attacks the frozen boundary.

## Required attacks

- tampered manifest, omitted transcript, extra transcript and reordered evidence;
- inconsistent session/startup/metadata/done identities and PID disagreement;
- qualified-but-divergent candidate and a candidate that passes a favorable subset;
- eligible infrastructure refusal that must not be counted as a pass;
- false or self-declared live provenance;
- changed descriptor/domain digest and changed policy digest;
- coverage inflation through duplicate or address-distinct records;
- missing diversity floor and optional-versus-required cell confusion;
- nondeterministic reports and non-UTF-8 evidence;
- mocked or synthetic E responses used without a label.

## Non-goals

- No certifier implementation, production policy change or native/live work.
- No change to the E result contract without a focused amendment.
- No claim that E1 is correct from its own tests or status.

## Stopping rules

- **Contract conflict:** if an attack exposes an ambiguous contract, propose a
  focused amendment; do not encode a private interpretation in a test.
- **Scope expansion:** do not build a second certification engine or a generic
  adversarial framework.
- **Evidence limitation:** publish the test design and the blocked final audit
  separately; do not mark the audit complete before E1 exists.

## Merge gate

E2's adversarial tests and audit report may merge before E1 completes, labelled as
preparation. E2's acceptance verdict is only valid after it runs against E1's
actual implementation.
